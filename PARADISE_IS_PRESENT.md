*Genre: Art rock / post-punk / ambient.*

## ALBUM NOTES

**All songs written in the present tense, on purpose.**

Recorded live where possible. The mistakes stayed in.

The title is not a self-help claim. It's a question in the form of a statement. Argue with it. That's the point.

*"Perception is the problem" is not an accusation. It is an invitation.*
